1. OBJECTIVE OF THIS PHASE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Verify participant count, class balance, sampling rate, physical channels, and
absence of missing values before designing any preprocessing or modeling steps.
Prevent ungrounded assumptions by analyzing the actual data file.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. VERIFIED SPECIFICATIONS & FINDINGS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Total Recorded Samples: 2,166,383 rows across 21 columns
* Total Participants (Children): 121
    - ADHD Group: 61 children (e.g., v1p, v10p, v12p...)
    - Healthy Control Group: 60 children (e.g., v1c, v2c, v3c...)
* Sampling Frequency (fs): 128 Hz
* Recording Length per Participant:
    - Minimum duration: 62.4 seconds (7,983 samples)
    - Maximum duration: 337.9 seconds (43,252 samples)
    - Mean duration: 139.9 seconds (17,904 samples)
* Scalp EEG Channels: Exactly 19 standard 10-20 channels verified:
    [Fp1, Fp2, F3, F4, C3, C4, P3, P4, O1, O2, F7, F8, T7, T8, P7, P8, Fz, Cz, Pz]
* Metadata Columns:
    - 'Class': Categorical label ('ADHD' or 'Control')
    - 'ID': Unique participant identifier string
* Missing Values (NaN / Nulls): Exactly 0 missing values detected across all channels.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. SIGNAL VISUALIZATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A 19-channel signal trace of participant v10p (ADHD) for the first 5 seconds was
plotted and saved to:
  results/figures/sample_raw_eeg.png

Key Observations:

- Central and parietal channels (Cz, Pz) exhibit continuous rhythmic oscillations.
- No flatlined or dead channels detected.
