1. SPLITTING METHODOLOGY & ANTI-LEAKAGE DESIGN
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Cross-Validation Scheme: StratifiedGroupKFold
* Number of Folds (K): 5
* Grouping Identifier: 'participant_id' (each child is treated as an indivisible group)
* Stratification Target: 'label' (ADHD: 1, Control: 0)
* Random Seed: 42 (strictly reproducible)
* Zero-Leakage Guarantee: Set intersection between train and test participants is EMPTY (0)
  for every single fold. No child ever appears in both training and test partitions.
