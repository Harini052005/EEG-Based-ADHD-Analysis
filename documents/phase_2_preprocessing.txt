1. PREPROCESSING SPECIFICATIONS & PARAMETERS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Sampling Frequency (fs): 128 Hz
* Raw Signal Calibration: Scaled by 0.1 (raw ADC -> true microvolts uV)
    - Clean baseline amplitude matches standard pediatric range: ~20-80 uV
* Continuous Zero-Phase Filtering:
    - Highpass Cutoff: 0.5 Hz (removes DC baseline wander and sweat drift)
    - Lowpass Cutoff: 30.0 Hz (Butterworth 4th-order forward-backward filtfilt)
    - Notch Filter: 50.0 Hz (Q=30.0, mains frequency attenuation)
    - Scientific Rationale: Preserves Theta (4-8 Hz), Alpha (8-13 Hz), and Beta (13-30 Hz)
      while attenuating high-frequency muscle (EMG) noise and mains hum.
* Epoching Strategy:
    - Epoch Duration: 2.0 seconds (256 samples at 128 Hz)
    - Window Overlap: 0% (strictly non-overlapping windows)
    - Filter Application: Continuous filtering applied BEFORE epoching to prevent
      boundary transient edge effects.
* Artifact Rejection Protocol:
    - Peak-to-Peak Amplitude Threshold: 150.0 uV
    - Any epoch with max(amplitude) - min(amplitude) > 150.0 uV on any channel
      is rejected as an ocular blink, movement burst, or muscle burst artifact.
